# Receipts

Receipts turns the real history between two people into a poem made only of things they actually said to each other, each line carrying proof of when and who.

## Source material

**Conversation**:
The full exchange between two people that a poem is drawn from: an exported WhatsApp chat, or a collection of letters.
_Avoid_: Chat log, history, corpus

**Message**:
One thing one person sent the other at one moment in a Conversation: a single chat message or a single letter.
_Avoid_: Text, post, entry

**Sender**:
The person who sent a Message.
_Avoid_: Author, speaker, user

**Snippet**:
A verbatim piece of a Message (a whole short Message, or one sentence of a long one); the smallest unit a poem can be built from.
_Avoid_: Quote, fragment, excerpt

## The poem

**Poem**:
An ordered set of 8 to 14 Lines, every one a real Snippet, chosen for an Occasion.
_Avoid_: Quilt, cento, story

**Line**:
A Snippet placed in a Poem.
_Avoid_: Verse, quote

**Receipt**:
The Sender and date shown under a Line, linking back to the original Message.
_Avoid_: Citation, source, timestamp

**Occasion**:
The reason the Poem is being made (birthday, anniversary, thank you, sorry, missing you, farewell), which steers which moments are chosen but never the wording.
_Avoid_: Event, theme, prompt

**Note**:
Beam's private one-line explanation of why it chose a Line, shown only to the Giver and never on the Card.
_Avoid_: Rationale, comment, caption

**Card**:
The finished, shareable rendering of a Poem with its Receipts.
_Avoid_: Quilt, output, artifact

## People

**Giver**:
The person who uploads a Conversation and makes the Poem.
_Avoid_: User, uploader, author

**Recipient**:
The person the Poem is made for, usually the other Sender in the Conversation.
_Avoid_: Reader, receiver

## Demo

**Gallery**:
The ready-made Conversation of Robert Browning and Elizabeth Barrett's 1845–1846 letters, from which judges can make Poems without uploading anything private.
_Avoid_: Demo data, sample
